**Machine Learning Researcher, Cleveland Clinic** · Jul 2026 – Present

Predicting sleep stages (wake, non-REM, REM) from Apple Watch heart rate and motion data using gradient-boosted models, validated against expert-scored EEG sleep staging. Currently studying whether predictions improve as more nights of per-person data become available.

### 🏠 AI-Powered Home Care Intake Platform
**Founder** · May 2026 – Present

An AI intake platform piloting with one location of a national home care franchise. It handles inbound calls, texts, and emails outside business hours, qualifies leads, books in-home assessments, and turns intake conversations into structured client profiles that care managers use to match caregivers and build schedules. Connects to each agencies CRM - writes structured profiles and bookings backing to the agency's CRM.
